# DESIGN.md

## Design Tokens & Craft Standard

- **Palette**: Legal Slate (`#070b14`, `#0c1322`, `#172440`), Deep Navy (`#233863`), Antique Gold (`#f59e0b`), Emerald Green (`#10b981`), Crimson Risk (`#ef4444`), Amber Warning (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography**: 
  - Headings: Serif (Georgia / Playfair / Cambria) for legal gravitas.
  - Body: Sans-serif (Inter / System) for data legibility.
  - Citations / Sections: Monospace (JetBrains Mono / Courier) for statutory provisions and citations.
- **Craft Principles**:
  - No generic 'AI slop' (no purple gradients over default cards).
  - Clear visual hierarchy for legal status: Established (Emerald), Disputed (Amber), Unsupported (Rose), Contradicted (Crimson).
  - High information density with collapsible depth and progressive disclosure.
