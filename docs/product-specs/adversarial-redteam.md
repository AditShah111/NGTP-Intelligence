# Adversarial Red-Team Specification

The Red-Team acts as senior Revenue Standing Counsel (DR / Special Counsel for GST Department):
1. **Section 155 Attack**: Taxpayer has not established actual receipt of goods (no weighbridge, no toll slips).
2. **Circular Trading & Fake Billing**: Supplier GSTIN cancelled ab-initio or non-existent at principal place of business.
3. **Non-reflection in GSTR-3B**: Section 16(2)(c) condition precedent of actual payment to government not satisfied.
4. **Time-bar under Section 16(4)**: ITC availed past statutory deadline prior to Finance Act 2024 retrospective amendment.
5. **Procedural Estoppel**: Failure to cross-examine supplier or raise objections in DRC-01 reply.
