========================================================================================================
STATEMENT OF FACTS
========================================================================================================

1. The Appellant is an infrastructure contractor holding valid GST registration bearing GSTIN: 27AAACS4321D1Z8.

2. In August 2019, the Appellant entered into an informal arrangement for procurement of building materials from M/s Global Trading Syndicate (GSTIN: 27AAHPG9988K1Z5).

3. The supplier issued Tax Invoice No. GTS/19-20/0118 dated 14-08-2019 for a total amount of INR 3,40,88,888/- including CGST of INR 26,00,000/- and SGST of INR 26,00,000/- (Total Tax: INR 52,00,000/-).

4. Due to extreme liquidity constraints and delays in client milestone disbursements, the Appellant could not pay the supplier within 180 days. The payment was eventually cleared via RTGS on 18-03-2020 (after 216 days from the invoice date). The Appellant did not reverse the credit in the interim period.

5. Furthermore, the supplier arranged delivery through local unorganized third-party transport. Consequently, no E-Way bills were generated in the portal, and no weighbridge slips or FASTag toll records are available with the Appellant.

6. The Respondent issued Show Cause Notice and subsequently passed Order-in-Original in Form GST DRC-07 dated 22-01-2024 disallowing ITC of INR 52,00,000/- under Section 16(2)(b) and Section 16(2)(c), demanding interest of INR 46,80,000/- under Section 50, and imposing 100% penalty of INR 52,00,000/- under Section 74(1) on allegations of circular trading and non-movement of goods.

========================================================================================================
GROUNDS OF APPEAL (DEFICIENT LEGAL PLEADING)
========================================================================================================

GROUND I: CLAIM OF BONA FIDE STATUS WITHOUT TRANSIT CORROBORATION
For that the Appellant possessed a tax invoice and paid the invoice value via banking channels on 18-03-2020. The Adjudicating Authority ought to have accepted the invoice as conclusive proof of supply. (Note: The pleading fails to rebut the total absence of E-Way bills or physical transit proofs under Section 16(2)(b) and Section 155).

GROUND II: RELIANCE ON SUNCRAFT ENERGY DESPITE PROVEN SHELL ENTITY FINDING
For that the Respondent should have proceeded against the supplier first under the ratio of Suncraft Energy. (Note: Unlike genuine matters where the seller was an active operating mill, here the Revenue physically proved the supplier was a non-existent 100 sq ft ghost entity with cancelled registration, rendering the transaction vulnerable to Malik Traders and Section 74).

GROUND III: FAILURE TO PLEAD SECTION 16(2) SECOND PROVISO REVERSAL
The Appellant admits that payment was made after 216 days, but pleads that late payment should not disentitle the Appellant from ITC once paid. (Note: The pleading concedes violation of the statutory 180-day mandate without proving compliance with interest under Section 50).

PRAYER
Wherefore, the Appellant prays that the Impugned Order dated 22-01-2024 may be quashed.
